## Cheating is a Widespread Use for GenAI in Education:

- The most common use of ChatGPT in education is for cheating on exams and assignments.

## Challenges in Detection:

- Subtle Mistakes: AI-generated errors are often subtle, making them hard to spot.
- Genuine References: References in AI-generated content are often real, adding to the challenge.
- Undetectable AI Content: detecting AI-generated text is nearly impossible.

## Least Useful Application:

- Although prevalent, using AI for cheating offers minimal educational value.

## Generative AI as a Learning Tool - Summarizer

Large Language Models are very good at summarizing text

## Use GenAI as a Summarizer Tool for Learning

- Adaptable Summaries: Tailors summaries to different levels of detail based on learning needs.
- Highlight Key Concepts: Helps pinpoint core ideas and main arguments quickly.
- Efficient Recap: Enables quick recap of essential information for reviewing material before exams or tests.
- Example (before watching a video or for recap):

## Example Prompt

I am attending a bootcamp on programming in Python. Please summarize the main points of this lecture in less than 500 words:

https://sback.github.io/shsg-summerschool2024/day-2/conditionals-asText

## Generative AI as a Learning Tool - Role Player

Large Language Models are very good at replicating style and impersonating figures

Use GenAI as a Role Player Tool for Learning

- Interactive Practice: Engage in real-time, simulated conversations with AI acting as a tutor or role-playing various scenarios.
- Flexible Roles: AI can assume various roles (e.g., language tutor, historical figure, business interview) to suit different learning contexts.
- Tailored Feedback: AI provides instant corrections and explanations, helping to improve comprehension.
- Safe Environment: Offers a non-judgmental space to practice skills or test knowledge, helping to build learner confidence.
- Example (learning a new language):

## Example Prompt

You are an Italian language tutor. I am a beginner student looking to improve my conversational skills. I want to practice discussing daily routines; please start by asking me a simple question in Italian. After I respond, IN ENGLISH correct my answer if needed and explain the correction. Then ask a follow-up question in Italian.

- https://resobscura.substack.com/p/simulatinghistory-with-chatgpt

## Generative AI as a Learning Tool - Flashcards

Large Language Models are very good at extracting key parts from material

## Use GenAI as a Flashcard Generator

- Instant Flashcards: AI can quickly convert lecture notes, readings, or summaries into flashcard questions.
- Customized Questions: Tailor flashcards to focus, e.g., on potential exam questions giving it past exams as prompt.
- Versatile Sources: Generate flashcards from various materials-class notes, readings, or even specific prompts.
- Export to Tools: Easily transfer generated questions into flashcard apps like Anki or Quizlet for ongoing study.
- Example (revisiting a lecture):

## Example Prompt

You are a university student revisiting a material for the written exam of a bootcamp on programming in Python. Write me a list of flashcard questions and model answers that I can put on flashcards to revise from, based on this lecture:

https://sback.github.io/shsg-summerschool2024/day-1/types-and-operations-asText

## Generative AI as a Learning Tool - Motivator

Large Language Models are very good at finding connections among different areas

## Use GenAI as a Motivator

- AI-Driven Learning Plans: Set personalized learning goals and track progress, providing regular feedback and encouragement to stay on course.
- Connecting Subjects to Goals: Explain why mastering certain topics is beneficial, tailored to your unique aspirations. For a student interested in sports, AI might explain how understanding cellular respiration can improve knowledge of nutrition and athletic performance.
- Boosts Motivation: Show real-world applications to make learning more meaningful and engaging.
- Encouraging Exploration: Suggest related topics or advanced concepts that align with a student's interests, fostering a deeper curiosity and desire to learn more.
- Example (revisiting a lecture):

## Example Prompt

Imagine you are a tutor for me: a student who is passionate about movies but needs to study linear algebra. Explain how understanding linear algebra relates to movies. Use examples from well-known films to make the connection clear and motivate me to engage with the subject.

## Generative AI as a Learning Tool - Practice Coach

Large Language Models are very good at generating relevant text and revising text

Use GenAI as a Practice Coach

- Homework and Assignment Feedback:
- Instant Grading: AI reviews your answers, identifies strengths and weaknesses, and provides detailed feedback.
- Personalized Improvement: Offers suggestions on how to improve answers, effectively acting as a free, personalized tutor.

## - Creating Tests and Mock Exams:

- Custom Exam Questions: AI can generate exam-style questions tailored to specific lectures or topics, helping you prepare thoroughly.

## - Future Possibilities:

- Ongoing Progress Tracking: By using the same chat, AI learns your weak areas and tailors feedback, making each practice session more effective.
- Example (preparing for an exam):

## Example Prompt

Please generate 10 exam-style questions that cover all the key topics from the [course name]. The questions should vary in difficulty. The format should include [multiple-choice, short answer, essay-style questions, …]. Ensure that the questions are aligned with the typical exam format for this subject, you can see here an example: [example].

## Generative AI as a Learning Tool - Exercise

1. Form Groups (2-3 minutes): Break into small groups of 3-4 students.
2. Choose Your Task (1 minute): Each group decided the task they want to work on.
- A. Task A: Use ChatGPT as a learning tool to better understand Generative AI by using one of the methods discussed in the "Circle of Voices" activity.
- B. Task B: Use ChatGPT as a learning tool to better understand a math-related topic from your first-year curriculum. Consider areas where you need clarification or deeper understanding.

## 3. Work on the Task (10 minutes):

- Collaborate within your group to explore the chosen task using ChatGPT.
- Take notes on how ChatGPT helps you understand the topic.
- Be ready to discuss what you learned and how effective ChatGPT was as a learning tool.

## 4. Present to the Plenum (5 minutes):

- Each group will briefly present their findings.
- Discuss how ChatGPT contributed to your understanding of the topic.
- Highlight any challenges or insights gained during the exercise.
